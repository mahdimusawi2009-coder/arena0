Neon Yard

A compact three-lane arena with a raised mezzanine, ramps on both flanks and cover crates for every range.

  • Mezzanine. Raised deck at the back with ramps on each side and glowing underpasses.
  • Cover. Nine crates with yellow tops or blue faces to peek and reload behind.
  • Pads. Four yellow pads for speed or jump boosts.
  • Spawns. Blue spawn front-left, yellow spawn back-right.
  • Night mode. Switch it on to see the neon light up the floor.
  • Players. A blue rifle player and a red sniper sprint, slide and fight. Rifle bursts and heavy sniper rounds fly, hit and spark. Use Follow blue to ride along.
  • Sound and blood. Synthesized gunshots, hits and slides (tap Sound to turn audio on). Stylized blocky blood, bleeding wounds and floor splats.
